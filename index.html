Who sees your personal data

Inside KNIK BIO, your enquiry goes to export sales first. An engineer reads it if the question is technical. If you ask for a works visit, the colleagues hosting you see your name and dates.

We operate from China, so personal data you send us is received and processed in Shanghai. Our hosting and email providers may also store it in other countries {hosting and email locations: confirm}. If you ask us to delete your personal data sooner, we will assess the request and act on it, except for records the law requires us to keep. We will tell you which records those are.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, the law may give you rights over your personal data. We will honour the following requests wherever we reasonably can, whether or not your local law requires it. You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your personal data
  • stop using it for a particular purpose, such as the newsletter
  • send you a copy you can take elsewhere, where the law gives you that right

You can also withdraw a consent at any time, by unsubscribing or by changing your cookie choice.

Email grace@kniktech.com with your request and the address you used when you wrote to us. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days. Where your law sets a different deadline, that deadline applies.

If you believe we have handled your personal data improperly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Cookies

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. This site uses two categories, and a third only if a video host is embedded.

On your first visit, a notice asks you to choose. Essential cookies work without your consent. Analytics cookies are set only if you accept. We do not use advertising cookies.

Links, WhatsApp and other sites

Some pages link to other sites, such as WhatsApp or a video host. Once you follow a link, that site's own policy applies. A message to our WhatsApp number is handled by WhatsApp under its terms until it reaches us, and by us from then on.

This site is written for business buyers. It is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

How we protect it

The site runs over HTTPS, so what you type travels encrypted. Access to enquiries is limited to the people dealing with them. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.
